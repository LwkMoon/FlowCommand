# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the project or to Windows development.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and keep discussion focused on the work.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information, including API keys, logs or transcripts that are not yours, without permission.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or repeated unwelcome contact.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may
temporarily or permanently ban people who repeatedly or seriously violate it. To report a problem, contact the maintainer
privately through the contact details on their GitHub profile.

This policy applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the project in public.
